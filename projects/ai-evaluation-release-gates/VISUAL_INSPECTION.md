## Committed hero

The public repository retains one reviewed 1440×960 Firefox render at `../../assets/project-previews/ai-evaluation-release-gates.png`.

The image shows the release-gate `HOLD` outcome, permanent synthetic/no-authority boundary, four-part evidence checklist, local receipt export, and the statement that the holdout is not confidential. The other walkthrough renders are verification/release artifacts rather than tracked repository files. Screenshot bytes are not treated as reproducible across browser/platform versions; visual inspection and the recorded digest/dimensions are the controls.
