# Capability demo script — 6 to 8 minutes

> INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO DEMO · SYNTHETIC DATA · NO AFFILIATION · NO PRODUCTION ACTION

## 0:00 — Set the boundary

Open `index.html?view=overview&scenario=baseline` at 1440×960.

Say: “This is an independent portfolio demo with synthetic data, no affiliation, and no production action. It demonstrates a generic implementation operating pattern and makes no claim about any vendor product or internal workflow.”

Point to the permanent disclosure and the first-value definition. Explain that the invented program is intentionally narrow: one operator validates one synthetic exception through reviewed resolution.

## 0:45 — Turn kickoff into decisions

Open **Discovery**. Confirm `DS-02`, the synthetic source-roster freeze. Enter a short delivery note and record it.

Say: “I want discovery to end in decisions with owners and exit criteria—not a transcript nobody can operate from. The note is escaped, kept in memory, and added only to this synthetic session audit.”

## 1:45 — Make readiness inspectable

Open **Readiness**, choose **Needs attention**, and inspect `RD-02`.

Advance it once to review-ready. Stop there.

Say: “The implementation lead can prepare work, evidence, and the next move. That does not silently become ready. A separate human review action is required, which makes handoffs and status reporting honest.”

Then use **Reviewer mark ready**.

## 3:00 — Reproduce and resolve an exception

Open **UAT + exceptions** and select `EX-17`.

Walk through observed versus expected behavior, impact, linked gates, reproduction steps, and the review condition. Advance from open to in progress, then review-ready, then use the explicitly labeled reviewer action.

Say: “The exception remains visible until the named reviewer accepts the evidence. Only then does the linked synthetic UAT case move to pass.”

## 4:30 — Transfer capability and ownership

Switch the scenario to **Review ready**, accept `EX-17`, then open **Enablement**. Record the missing `HO-03` support-owner acknowledgment.

Say: “Attendance is useful context, but demonstrated practice and a receiving owner are stronger adoption signals. The handoff bundles the runbook, known exceptions, and first-week cadence.”

## 5:30 — Make go-live a deliberate decision

Open **Acceptance** and run the checks. The score should be `8/9`; the system still awaits named human acceptance.

Use **Record synthetic go-live acceptance**. The score becomes `9/9` and the audit preview records the decision.

Say: “This is deliberately scoped to the invented demo fixture. It is not production authorization, a deployment, or an external system action.”

## 6:45 — Close with the audit

Open the audit trail and export the local JSON.

Say: “The export happens only after my click, stays local, and describes its own boundary: synthetic, independent, unaffiliated, and without production authority. Reloading resets the entire app because there is no persistence.”

## Interview follow-ups

- **How would this change for a real customer?** Replace invented fixtures only after authorization; bind each readiness item to the customer's actual source, owner, and acceptance language.
- **How do you keep Product and Engineering useful?** Send a compact repro with observed/expected, impact, fixture, severity, owner, and a testable review condition.
- **How do you prevent status theater?** Separate prepared, review-ready, reviewer-accepted, and go-live-accepted states; show blockers in the rollup instead of averaging them away.
- **What would you measure?** Time to first value, milestone predictability, exception aging, practice completion, receiving-owner acknowledgment, and early-life support demand—each with an explicit source and scope.
