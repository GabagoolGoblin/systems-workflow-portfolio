# Community conduct

Any interaction around this public portfolio must remain focused, respectful,
and professional. Participants must critique work rather than people, respect
stated claim and data boundaries, and avoid harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or disclosure of another person's
private information.

The maintainer may hide, lock, or remove interactions that violate this policy
and may block or report an account when necessary.

Do not publish sensitive conduct details. Use GitHub's platform reporting and
blocking controls for abuse. Issues are disabled on this repository and pull
requests are not reviewed.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
